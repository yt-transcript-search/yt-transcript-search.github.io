Quick start

Up and running in under a minute

01
Install

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons. No account required

02
Open a video

Go to any YouTube video with captions and click the extension icon

03
Load Transcript

Click Load Transcript. The full timed Transcript appears in seconds

04
Search & jump

Type any word or phrase. Click a result to jump straight to that moment

Interface

The overlay at a glance

The panel is movable (drag the top bar) and resizable (drag edges or corners). The default theme on first load is Deep Ocean

The overlay in the default Deep Ocean theme, with its main areas labelled

  • 1. Top bar — Logo, website & YT channel links, Settings/Help (⚙), Opacity slider, Theme picker, Layout A/B slots and Minimise
  • 2. URL bar — Active YT video URL updated automatically, ‘URL Lock (Pro)’ and ‘Load Transcript’
  • 3. Video info row — Title, YT channel, Duration followed by ‘Import Chapters’ button (where applicable)
  • 4. Transcript viewer (left panel) — Search field, Search history selector, Case toggle, Search, Clear, View mode, Font size, Teleprompter (Pro), Timestamp Links/Selection Copy toggle, Sync offsets, Playback speed, Minimap show/hide, the loaded Transcript, Follow, Playback controls and In/Out Looping
  • 5. Timestamp Links viewer (right panel) — Colour-coded search batches and manual entries, with Select All/None, Delete, Undo/Redo
  • 6. Bottom bar — Import (Pro), Export, Pro licence button and Noticeboard

Prefer the panels swapped? Just click the ⇄ button at the top of the divider between them. Everywhere on this website, “left” and “right” refer to the default arrangement with the Transcript panel on the left

Loading a Transcript

Straight from YouTube’s captions

Transcripts come from YouTube’s own caption data. On a watch page, click Load Transcript. You can also paste any watch URL into the URL field

  • Once loaded, title, channel and duration appear. Click any Transcript line to seek the video
  • Language / caption-track picker ★ Pro — Long-press Load Transcript to choose among available languages and tracks (official, auto-generated, etc.)
  • URL lock ★ Pro — Long-press the lock icon. When locked, the URL and Transcript stay frozen even if you navigate to another video. Useful for screen capture or multi-tab comparison

Language selection menu — choose the caption track that best matches the video

Searching

Find every occurrence instantly

Type a term or phrase and press Enter or click Search. Matches are highlighted in the Transcript with colour-coded backgrounds that match each search batch

  • Case sensitivity — Toggle with the Aa button
  • Whole-word matching — Available as a persisted preference
  • Search (A) / Append mode ★ Pro — Long-press Search to toggle. When active, the button shows “Search (A)” and new results are added to the existing Timestamp Links list instead of replacing it
  • History — Recall previous queries. Pro users get a longer history depth
  • Undo/Redo — Ctrl+Z steps back through search states; Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y steps forward
Timestamp Links

Build a curated list of moments

Every search creates a coloured batch in the Timestamp Links panel (on the right by default). Manually added lines appear under a “MANUALLY CREATED” header

  • Checkboxes control which entries are included when you export Timestamp Links
  • Select All/None, Delete and Undo/Redo
  • Click a header arrow to expand or collapse a batch; click any entry to seek the video
  • Add a Transcript line manually to the list from the export context menu ★ Pro
  • Bookmark ★ Pro — When you jump to a Timestamp Link, the place you were watching is saved as a blue Bookmark, shown under any Chapters (or at the top of the list if there are none). Click it to go straight back, or right-click it to move it to where you are now, remove it, or jump to it. While you keep jumping around, the Bookmark stays where it is. A new one is only saved after you have watched on for about 30 seconds from a new place. Nothing is saved if you had barely started watching or the jump is within 5 seconds of where you were. It is not ticked, exported, counted or deleted with the other entries and it is cleared when you clear the list or change video
  • Import ★ Pro — Load a Timestamp Links file (.json) that was shared with you, made with Export → Links as JSON. You can also drag the file onto the overlay
View & playback

Read, follow and control the video

  • View modes — Toggle Line-by-line and Continuous text
  • Font size — Size slider, or Ctrl + scroll over the Transcript. Ctrl+0 or the reset button restores default
  • Minimap ★ Pro — Vertical strip of coloured dots for every Timestamp Link match. Click a dot to jump. Show/hide with the ▾ button at the far right of the video-info row (Title / Channel / Duration). Preference is remembered
  • Sync offset — Coarse (seconds) and fine (ms) sliders when captions lag or lead the audio. Double-click a slider to reset. The same offsets apply to Teleprompter and export
  • Following — Transcript auto-scrolls to keep the current spoken line in view
  • Play / Pause / Stop — Control the video from the overlay
  • [ In ] / [ Out ] — Mark a range for focused listening or looping
  • Selection Copy — Click the 🔗 Timestamp Links / 📝 Selection Copy button to switch from “click to seek” to free text selection, then paint over the part of the Transcript you want. When you let go, a small menu offers Copy including the times, Copy excluding the times or Cancel. Press Enter for your last choice or Esc to cancel
  • Selection Copy with Ctrl ★ Pro — No need to switch modes: hold Ctrl and paint over the text. The menu appears when you release Ctrl, so you can repaint until the selection is exactly right and the overlay returns to Timestamp Links mode afterwards
Themes, opacity & layout

Make it yours

  • Themes — Open the palette button for multiple carefully designed themes (light, dark and specialised colourways). Default on first load is Deep Ocean. Choice is remembered
  • Opacity — Let the video show through the panel to any degree
  • Layout slots A & B — Short-press to recall a saved position and size; long-press to save the current layout. If you have saved a layout in slot A, it is applied automatically each time the page loads
  • Minimise — Collapse to a small circular icon; click again to restore
Teleprompter

Scroll in sync with the video ★ Pro

Opens a dedicated floating panel that scrolls the Transcript with the video — ideal for voice-overs, practice, or following along without the main overlay

  • Click Teleprompter in the main panel. Hover the teleprompter to reveal gear (settings) and close
  • Settings update live and are saved automatically. Use 1-column or 2-column layout; position is remembered per mode

Choose whichever layout suits you. Both offer the same settings: profiles A / B / C, Lines, Text, Cascade, Background, Active-line highlight and Sync offset

  • Lines Before / After — Surrounding lines around the active line
  • Font, colour, bold, text shadow — Full appearance control
  • Scale font with panel — Font grows or shrinks as you resize the panel
  • Cascade — Non-active lines scale down and fade with distance
  • Background — Optional solid background with colour and opacity
  • Active line highlight & border — Emphasise the current line
  • Anchor to video — Panel follows the video player while you scroll the page (Normal/Cinema only)
  • Profiles (A / B / C) — Short-press to recall, long-press to save the current settings and position. For example, you could use each profile for a different YouTube view mode (Normal, Cinema or Fullscreen)

Examples of different teleprompter appearances — high-contrast dark panels to bright highlighted styles

Export

Take your results with you

Click Export which shows two columns: Full Transcript and Timestamp Links. When a sync offset is active, you can choose adjusted or original timestamps

Export menu — Full Transcript choices on the left and Timestamp Links on the right. Also the means to choose between saving synced vs original timestamps

  • Full Transcript — Plain text and clipboard free; SRT, CSV, JSON, Markdown Pro
  • Timestamp Links — Text (.txt) and clipboard free; JSON and Markdown Pro; add line to list Pro
  • Synced or original times — When a sync offset is active, choose whether the exported times are the adjusted (synced) ones or the original ones from YouTube
  • Only checked Timestamp Links entries are included when exporting links
Free vs Pro

Fully usable free; Pro unlocks the advanced tools

Free includes: Load and searching Transcripts, Colour-coded Timestamp Links, Limited Export options, Theme choice, Following mode

Pro unlocks: Teleprompter (full customisation and profiles), Append Search, URL lock, Language picker, Import chapters, Manual add to Timestamp Links, Bookmark, Import of shared Timestamp Links files, Selection Copy with Ctrl, SRT/CSV/JSON/Markdown export, Longer history and undo depth, Minimap and much more

Activate Pro with the ★ Pro button — enter the email used at purchase and your licence key. Verification is local and cryptographic

Tips

Work faster

  • Combine Append Search with colour batches to build a multi-topic Timestamp Links list for one video
  • Use Layout A for a compact side panel and Layout B for a larger research view
  • Fix sync offset once — it applies to seeking, Following, Teleprompter and exports
  • Lock the URL for screen recording or comparing Transcripts across tabs
  • In Teleprompter, enable “Scale font with panel” and resize until the text feels right for your distance
  • The minimap is especially useful on long videos with many scattered matches
  • Export Markdown or JSON (Pro) when you want to process results in other tools
After an update

If the extension stops responding after an update, refresh the YouTube tab. That reloads the content script with the new version
