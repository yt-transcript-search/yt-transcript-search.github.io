Quick Start

Four steps

STEP 01
Pin the extension

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin beside YT Transcript Search, so it's always one click away

STEP 02
Open any YouTube video

Any video with captions: lectures, podcasts, tutorials, interviews, music videos

STEP 03
Click the extension icon

The overlay appears and loads the full Transcript, ready to search

STEP 04
Search and jump

Type any word or phrase. Click a result and the video jumps to that exact moment

This is what you've just added to YouTube. The Transcript is on the left and your colour-coded Timestamp Links on the right. You can swap the two panels with the ⇄ button on the divider
