Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours

Last updated: June 2026

YT Transcript Search is built to need as little of your data as possible. Here's exactly what that means in practice — no legal padding, just what actually happens.

🔒
No account, ever

No sign-up, no email required to use the free version, no login wall.

🖥️
Nothing sent to our servers

We don't run a server that the extension talks to. There's nothing for us to collect.

📺
Transcripts come from YouTube

The extension fetches transcript data directly from YouTube's own API. It never passes through us.

💳
Payment handled by PayPal

We never see or store your card or payment details. PayPal handles that entirely.

What We Collect

In short: nothing, unless you buy Pro

Using the free version of YT Transcript Search involves no data collection on our part at all. The extension runs entirely inside your browser tab, fetching transcript data directly from YouTube and storing your preferences (theme, panel position, that kind of thing) only on your own device.

If you choose to buy Pro, you send a one-time payment via PayPal and include your email address in the payment note. We use that email solely to send your licence key, and to verify the key if you contact us about activation. We don't store it anywhere beyond our own email inbox, and we don't add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone.

What Stays On Your Device

Local storage, explained

The extension uses your browser's local storage (e.g, 'chrome.storage.local' for Chrome users) to remember your settings between sessions. None of this ever leaves your device — it's not synced to any account and we have no access to it.

What's stored locally Why
Chosen theme, panel position & size So the overlay looks and sits the way you left it
Timestamp Link collections Kept for your current session, per video
Pro licence status & email So you don't have to re-activate every time you open a video
Teleprompter & Follow settings (Pro users) Your preferred font size, colours, columns, and sync offset
Noticeboard read status So you only see "new" badges for messages you haven't read yet
How Transcripts Are Fetched

Straight from YouTube. Nothing in between

When you click Load Transcript, the extension asks YouTube's own API for that video's caption data — the same data YouTube itself uses to show captions. The request goes straight from your browser to YouTube; it never touches any server we run, because we don't run one.

The transcript lives in memory for as long as you're viewing it, and is discarded the moment you navigate away or close the tab. We have no way of seeing what videos you've searched, what you've searched for, or what you've exported.

Third-Party Services

Only two, and you already know them

YouTube / Google. The extension runs on YouTube's website and fetches data from YouTube's API. Your use of YouTube itself is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not ours — we're just a layer on top of a page you're already on.

PayPal. If you buy Pro, your payment is processed entirely by PayPal. We never see your card details or PayPal password. See PayPal's Privacy Statement for how they handle your information.

We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services of our own. There's no third-party script in the extension beyond what's needed to talk to YouTube's API.

Questions

A few specific ones

Do you use cookies or trackers?

No. The extension doesn't set cookies and contains no tracking or analytics code.

Do you sell or share my data?

We have none to sell. The only personal information we ever hold is a Pro buyer's email, used solely to deliver a licence key, and never shared with anyone.

What happens if I uninstall the extension?

Everything stored locally — your theme, settings, Timestamp Links, Pro status — is removed along with the extension. If you reinstall later, just activate Pro again with the same email and key.

Is this policy different for the Pro version?

No. Pro unlocks extra features inside the same extension; it doesn't change what data is collected or how it's handled.

Who do I contact about my data?

Email yt.transcript.search@gmail.com with any question, including requests to delete your email from our inbox after a Pro purchase.

Changes To This Policy

If anything changes, this page will

If we ever start collecting data we don't collect today, this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above. We won't change what we collect without saying so here.

Questions about your data?

We're happy to answer anything this page didn't cover.
